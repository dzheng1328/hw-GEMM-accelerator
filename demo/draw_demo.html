The math here mirrors model/evaluate_quantized.py, which replicates the datapath proven bit-exact against the RTL in tb/mnist/test_mnist.py: quantize the 8×8 input (clip(round(px/s_in))), int8 matmul → int32 accumulate, ReLU on the raw accumulator, one fixed requantize to int8, second int8 matmul, argmax over the 10 raw int32 logits. Confidence is a softmax over the dequantized logits. The quantized model scores 94.82% on the full 10,000-image MNIST test set.
